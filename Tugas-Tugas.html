The Perfect Family

Perfect = looking back

All perfect tenses use a form of HAVE: have / has, had, or will have. The sentence stands at one point in time (the past, now, or the future) and looks back at something that happened before it.

V3 = finished; the focus is the result.   been + V-ing = still going on; the focus is how long.

Tip: the helping verb shows the TIME (had = past, have/has = present, will have = future). Do you see been + V-ing? Then the tense is continuous.

The six tenses, one by one

The examples come from Group 1's story, Rafi's PKL Diary.

Perfect or Perfect Continuous?

Perfect (V3): result, how many

Rafi has fixed three motorcycles today.

The work is finished. The result is what matters.

Perfect Continuous: activity, how long

Rafi has been fixing motorcycles for three hours.

The length of the activity is what matters.

Careful: some verbs are not used in the continuous: know, like, love, want, believe, have (= to own).
✓ I have known Rafi for years.   ✗ I have been knowing Rafi for years.
